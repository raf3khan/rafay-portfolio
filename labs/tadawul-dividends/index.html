How the dates work. Tadawul trades Sunday to Thursday and settles trades two trading days later (T+2). Companies announce an eligibility date. The ex-date is one trading day before it, so you need to buy by the close of the trading day before the ex-date. "Last day to buy" skips weekends but not public holidays, so check the company's announcement around Eid and National Day.

Yields. "This payment" is the dividend per share divided by the latest close. "Trailing" uses dividends paid over the last twelve months. Yields are left blank where no recent price is on file.

Not investment advice. Always confirm dates in the company's announcement on Saudi Exchange.

Your rate joins the ranking above as "Your rate". One basis point (bp) is 0.01% of the trade value.

Where the 0.155% goes

The CMA caps the total commission on Saudi equities at 15.5 basis points (0.155%) of the trade value, charged on both the buy and the sell. Only the broker's share can be discounted. The rest goes to the market and the regulator on every trade, even at a zero commission broker. There has been no fixed minimum per order on Tadawul since July 2016.

Why small US orders cost more

Saudi brokers charge US stocks per share with a minimum per order, so a small order pays the minimum and its cost as a share of the trade climbs fast. 15% VAT applies on top of the commission. Converting SAR to USD may carry an FX spread that brokers don't publish clearly, so enter your own. US sell orders also pay small SEC and FINRA fees, which are not included here.

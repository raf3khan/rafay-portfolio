Where the 0.155% goes

The CMA caps the total commission on Saudi equities at 15.5 basis points (0.155%) of the trade value, charged on both the buy and the sell. Only the broker's share can be discounted. The rest goes to the market and the regulator on every trade, even at a zero commission broker. There has been no fixed minimum per order on Tadawul since July 2016.

Why small US orders can cost more

Several Saudi brokers now offer free US trading, often on a basic plan. Paid plans charge per share with a minimum per order, so a small order pays the minimum and its cost as a share of the trade climbs fast. 15% VAT applies on top of any commission. Converting riyals to dollars may carry an FX spread that brokers don't publish clearly, so enter your own. US sell orders also pay small SEC and FINRA fees, which are not included here.

About these tools

I'm Rafay Khan, a product designer and product manager who builds digital products for banks and capital markets in Riyadh. Labs is where I build the tools I wish existed for investors and borrowers in the region.

Each tool says where its data comes from and when it was last checked. Market data comes from public sources such as Saudi Exchange disclosures and SEC filings. Rules come from the official programme or regulator. None of it is investment advice, so always confirm with the company announcement or your bank.

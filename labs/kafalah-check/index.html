      How Kafalah works

      You don't apply to Kafalah on your own. You apply for financing at a participating bank or finance company, and the lender asks the SME Bank for a guarantee on part of the loan. If the business can't repay, the guarantee covers the lender for that share. The lender still makes its own credit decision, so its own conditions apply on top of the rules below.

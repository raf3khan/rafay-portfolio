How I work

From a customer's problem to a product in production

The same three steps sit behind every case study on this page.

01

Understand and frame

Sit with customers, relationship managers and operations. Map the journey as it really runs today, then agree one north star metric and the guardrails with risk and compliance.

02

Prototype and test

Wireframe and prototype fast, test with real users, and let A/B tests and behavior data decide what ships.

03

Ship and measure

Deliver in agile increments, track the KPIs we committed to, and keep improving after launch.
